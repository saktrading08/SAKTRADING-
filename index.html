      How to read it

      Live: 7-day pump.fun + PumpSwap volume at or above 1.5× the prior 30-day average. Warming: 1.3× to 1.5×. Dead: at or below 0.7×. Neutral: in between. The SOL trend is a filter: regimes have run far more often with SOL above its 50-day average and not already up 30%+ in a month.

      Sources: DefiLlama DEX volume, stablecoin and price APIs (CoinGecko-backed). Memecoin volume data starts April 2024 (pump.fun) and March 2025 (PumpSwap).
