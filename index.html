      How to read it

      Staging = 7-day net stablecoin growth at least one standard deviation above its 90-day norm while SOL's 14-day realized vol sits in the bottom third of its last year, for two days running. Deploying = combined pump.fun, PumpSwap and Raydium volume above 1.75× its 30-day median. Extended = vol in the top 30% with no fresh inflow. Everything else is Quiet.

      Sources: DefiLlama stablecoin and DEX volume APIs, DefiLlama price API (CoinGecko-backed). Raydium carries non-meme volume too; pump.fun data starts April 2024, PumpSwap March 2025.
